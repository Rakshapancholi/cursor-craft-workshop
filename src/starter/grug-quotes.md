# Grug quotes

A one-page web app. One quote a day. Grug keep it simple.

# Visual rules
Colour: ink #1C140C · paper #EDE4D4 · accent #C45C26 · mute #6E5C4A
Type: Syne for the quote. Outfit for the date. Never Inter.
Space: 8px grid. Page gutter 32px. Quote is the page. Gaps not sibling margins.
Radius: 0. No rounded cards.
Icons: none. Words only.
Voice: short, blunt, no “inspire” or “unlock.”
Never: gradient, glass, stock portraits, share rows, fake author photos, more than one button.

# Product
The quote is the page. Date above. Author below, small.
One ghost button: another. It swaps the quote. No feed, no archive, no search.
Empty: no quote today. come back.
Done = I can read today’s quote without scrolling, and tap another.
