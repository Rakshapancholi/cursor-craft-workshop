# Visual rules

Colour: ink #0B0B12 · paper #F7F5FF · accent #7C6CF0 · mute #6B6680
Type: Syne for display, Outfit for body. Never Inter.
Space: 8px grid. Gutters 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 by breakpoint. Gaps not sibling margins.
Radius: 12px on buttons, 20px on cards. No other radii.
Icons: one set, line, 24px. No emoji as icons.
Voice: first person, specific, no “seamless” or “unlock.”
Never: gradient text, glassmorphism, fake metrics, stock portraits,
more than one shadow, centred body copy on desktop.

# Components

Button: filled accent or ghost. One height. No extra variants today.
Card: 20px radius, 24px padding, hairline border, no hover circus.
