# Weather

A one-page web app. One place. Today’s weather.

# Visual rules
Colour: ink #0B1C2A · paper #F2F6F8 · accent #2F7D9A · mute #5D7380
Type: Syne for the temperature. Outfit for place and condition. Never Inter.
Space: 8px grid. Gutter 32px. Temperature is the display. Gaps not sibling margins.
Radius: 12px on the one card. No other radii.
Icons: one set, line, 24px. One condition icon. No emoji.
Voice: plain. “Rain this afternoon.” Not “be prepared for anything.”
Never: gradient sky, glass cards, fake AQI, 10-day carousel, glow, a tips paragraph.

# Product
Place name. Big temperature. Short condition. High / low in mute.
One card. No hourly strip.
Done = I know if I need a jacket without hunting.
